I am a medical doctor and Cambridge Information Engineering graduate, currently pursuing an MSc in AI in Biomedicine and Healthcare at UCL, supported by the Sparck AI Scholarship. 

My interests lie in machine learning, computational neuroscience and medical devices. 

My previous research includes developing Bayesian models of learning using behavioural data from people with substance-use disorders at the University of Cambridge, and computational analysis of movement-video and microscopy data at the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology. I subsequently completed two years of NHS foundation training, including experience in intensive care, acute medicine and general surgery. This gave me firsthand insight into the limitations of existing healthcare technologies and the opportunities for  rigorous innovation. 

I am now strengthening my foundations in machine learning, reinforcement learning and mathematical modelling, with the aim of contributing directly to the development of intelligent systems. I am particularly interested in research and engineering opportunities in AI/ML, software and medical technology, including opportunities in Japan and the UK

I am currently working with Dr Tim Bonnici at UCL on the concept of a 'good death' in the ICU - developing a framework to assess this, as well as developing a statistical comparative study between Japan and the UK. 

Hobbies-wise: I am currently very much into 'guerilla philosophy' and have attended numerous workshops around London! I am exploring the concept of suffering, normalisation and medicalisation!
